  • 1. The Key (20-nt gRNA): Guides Cas9 to search for matching DNA sequences.
  • 2. The Clasp (PAM): Cas9 first locks onto an NGG sequence in DNA before testing the key.
  • 3. Auto-Fix Optimizer: Click "Optimize Key" if look-alikes appear to auto-select safer targets.

Plain-Language Key Sensitivity Summary
Seed End (Positions 13–20): High Sensitivity to Typos Errors near the PAM clasp prevent Cas9 from unwinding the DNA helix. A single typo here severely drops fit score by 85%–98%, rendering cutting weak or impossible.
Tail End (Positions 1–12): Low Sensitivity to Typos Errors at the distal tail are widely tolerated by Cas9. Typos here cause only a minor 15%–35% drop in cutting score, meaning look-alikes with tail errors remain dangerous.
