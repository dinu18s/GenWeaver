How CRISPR "Lock & Key" Works

  • 1. The Key (20-nt gRNA): The guide RNA that guides Cas9 to search for matching DNA sequences.
  • 2. The Clasp (PAM): Cas9 first locks onto an NGG sequence in the DNA before testing if the key fits.
  • 3. The Seed Lock (Pos 13-20): Mismatches in these 8 letters prevent Cas9 from opening the DNA and cutting.
  • 4. Click Any Hit Row: Click any detected hit in the results table below to view the interactive Lock-and-Key Visual Fit Inspector!
